L'Art de Vivre & Digital Culinary Craft

Welcome to La Table Française, an immersive celebration of authentic French cuisine and gastronomic heritage, crafted with care by Team AC2 at SRM University.

Bringing together creative web engineering, rich cultural research, and interactive 3D digital experiences, our team designed this platform to let you explore France's timeless cooking culture, step-by-step techniques, and regional specialties with elegance and ease.
